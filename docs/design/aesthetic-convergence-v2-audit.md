# Aesthetic convergence V2: baseline audit and plan

Baseline: canonical 89a9e4f, installed build inspected on this PC at its default window size. Real Ready, healthy Setup, 22 installed models, unmeasured Optimization, and About were viewed before edits. Loading states were also observed. Other states require isolated fixtures or post-build checks.

## Findings

- The warm paper/ink palette and Ready headline already establish the right identity. The rail lockup is small; page buttons have no persistent selected state. The executable lacks an embedded icon.
- Ready opens a large raw activity area even while its link says “Show details”. This makes the completed state look like an internal control panel and wastes most of the main canvas. On wide windows, useful content stays left while empty space grows right.
- Setup has clear truthful prerequisite rows, but its actions remain flat stock rectangles and the current next action is not visually separated from inspection tools.
- Models & fit has useful dense data, but the table uses less than the available width, truncates long model names and “RAM-assisted”, and the evidence is a small unstructured text box. The hardware identity is a sentence rather than a scan-friendly summary.
- Optimization preserves the right semantic distinction in copy, but an unmeasured state devotes the largest surface to a nearly empty text box. The recommendation, current setting, and override controls do not read as separate decisions.
- About is visually quiet, but its upstream-affiliation disclaimer and legacy repository links distract from AFK AI's product identity. Keep only the product name, version/channel, local-first promise, canonical Source and Support links, and access to installed licence notices.

## Convergence plan

1. Establish shared button, navigation, focus, and sunken-detail treatments in the existing theme; embed a multi-resolution icon derived from the current A mark.
2. Make Home's activity conditional on disclosure, retain Ready and Open Chat as the leading hierarchy, and balance content width across normal and wide windows.
3. Keep the dense model list, improve column allocation and row selection, group machine and recommendation facts, and turn selected-model evidence into a readable sheet with selectable technical data.
4. Give Optimization an intentional unmeasured state and a compact comparison for measurements while keeping current setting and user override visibly separate.
5. Apply the same hierarchy to Setup, then inspect default, minimum, wide, keyboard, and available DPI states; run native and repository gates before integration.
